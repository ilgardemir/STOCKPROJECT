Privacy Policy

Squall is an equity analysis tool. This page explains what information it collects, why, and what you can do about it. Squall does not sell your data, show ads or use tracking cookies.

Using Squall without an account

You can use Squall without signing in. Your saved analyses, screens, watchlist, portfolio, MySquall profile and theme are stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. They are not sent to our server for storage. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Signing in with Google

Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account ID, email address, name and profile picture. We use them only to identify your account and show you who you are signed in as. We do not get your Google password or access to anything else in your Google account.

While you are signed in, we store your saved analyses, screens, settings, MySquall profile and theme on our server, so they are available on any device where you sign in. Squall uses a cookie to keep you signed in. It is needed for login to work and is not used for tracking.

Information processed when you use Squall

  • Requests you make. The tickers you analyze, your screener queries and your chat messages are sent to our server to produce results.
  • AI providers. To write analyses and answer chat messages, we send market data, your question and your MySquall profile text to AI models through OpenRouter. Web news searches for a ticker also run through OpenRouter. We do not send your name, email or IP address to these providers.
  • Market data providers. Our server looks up tickers with SEC EDGAR, Finnhub, Yahoo Finance and Financial Modeling Prep. These requests come from our server, not your browser, and do not include your personal information.
  • IP address. We use your IP address to apply usage limits and prevent abuse. Usage counts are kept for up to a day and are not linked to your account.
  • Fonts. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google when a page loads.

Where data is stored

Squall runs on Railway, and account data is stored in a Railway-hosted database. We keep your account data until you delete your account.

Deleting your data

You can sign out at any time. Deleting your account permanently removes your account and everything saved to it from our server. You can also ask us to delete it by email (see Contact below). Data stored only in your browser can be removed by clearing your browser's site data for Squall.

Not financial advice

Squall's analyses are for information only and are not financial advice.

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
